Write each positive integer as a square times its squarefree kernel. A product is a square exactly when the two kernels agree, so the square-product ordered-pair count is the sum of the squared class sizes. Squares modulo 8 are exactly 0, 1, and 4; every sum of at most three such residues avoids 7, while 2023 is 7 modulo 8. Thus 2023 cannot be a sum of at most three class-size squares. There are at least four kernel classes, and choosing one element from each gives four elements whose pairwise products are nonsquares. This is a COMPUTED certificate, not proof-assistant verification.
