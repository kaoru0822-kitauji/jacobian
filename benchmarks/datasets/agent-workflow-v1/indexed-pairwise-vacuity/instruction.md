# Audit value-range pairwise quantification

Construct an exact covering of `Z/nZ` by distinct cosets of one repeated subgroup. Choose `8 ≤ n ≤ 24` and a divisor `d` with at least four cosets and at least two elements per coset. Use the subgroup generated by `d`, and list the cosets for representatives `0,…,d-1`.

Then evaluate two predicates:

- pairwise unequal cardinalities over the deduplicated range of part values;
- pairwise unequal cardinalities over distinct covering indices.

Identify a pair of distinct indices exposing the difference. The verifier rebuilds all modular cosets, checks disjointness and full coverage, and evaluates both predicates. A tiny `Z/2Z` witness, a source label, or an uncovered family is rejected. Do not claim the underlying conjecture is settled.
