Finite moment checks cannot justify the universal exponent claim. With a=1, take h(z)=1+z, zeta=t/(1-t), and v(z)=-1-(3/2)z-(1/2)z^2. Exact substitution gives zeta=t h(zeta) and 1-2t v(zeta)=(1-t)^(-2). The constant-term-one formal square root contributes 1-t, which cancels 1-t h'(zeta). Hence the ordinary generating function is 1 and, for Q=Z, the mixed generating function is t/(1-t). Coefficient extraction gives E(P^m)=0 and E(QP^m)=m! for every m>=1. This is a COMPUTED formal-identity certificate under the frozen premise, not machine verification of the paper or a result about dimension two.
