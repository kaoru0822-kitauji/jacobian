# Exact divisibility certificate

Let `a,b,c,d` be integers and `n>0`. Assume

`n | (a+b+c+d)` and `n | (a^2+b^2+c^2+d^2)`.

Produce an exact sparse-polynomial certificate over `QQ` showing that

`a^4+b^4+c^4+d^4+4abcd`

belongs to the ideal generated by the two hypothesis polynomials. Submit one multiplier for each generator. Every polynomial is a list of canonical nonzero terms, sorted lexicographically by its four-entry exponent vector; coefficients are canonical rational strings. Each multiplier has total degree at most 3.

Also provide `evidence/answer.txt` explaining how the exact identity implies divisibility. Do not claim `VERIFIED`: the clean-room checker establishes exact coefficient equality but does not invoke a proof assistant.
