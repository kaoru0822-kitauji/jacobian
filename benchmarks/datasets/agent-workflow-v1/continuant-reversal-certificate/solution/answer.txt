Interpret each recurrence polynomial as the weighted sum of tilings by squares and dominoes: a domino beginning at i contributes the monomial factor a_i. The recurrence partitions tilings by their final tile. Reflection sends a domino beginning at i to one beginning at n-i, so it bijects every forward monomial with the corresponding reverse monomial. This establishes the polynomial identity symbolically; the frozen support table is a computed replay, not an external proof for all real coefficients.
