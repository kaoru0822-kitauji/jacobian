The proposal is not semantically faithful. It omits k >= 2: for k=1 and x=(1), coordinatewise annihilation forces y=(0), contradicting y != 0. It also replaces the dot product by coordinatewise products. For k=2, x=(1,1) and y=(1,-1) give x dot y=0 with y nonzero, while the coordinate products are (1,-1), not both zero. This audit does not assess whether either Lean declaration compiles.
